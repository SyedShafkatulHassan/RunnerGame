About Shafkat Runner

Shafkat Runner is a free endless runner you can play straight in your browser, on a phone, tablet or computer. It needs no download and no account.

You play as Bolt, a small rooftop robot who loves running across the city skyline at night. Jump crates and vents, leap across the gaps between buildings, and duck under patrol drones to see how far you can go.

Original game

Shafkat Runner's character, artwork, sounds and name are original. Every graphic is drawn in code, and every sound is generated in the browser. The text font is Fredoka, which is free under the SIL Open Font Licence.
