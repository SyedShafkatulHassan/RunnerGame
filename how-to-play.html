How to play Shafkat Runner

Shafkat Runner is a free endless runner. Bolt, a small rooftop robot, runs on his own across a neon city at night. You only control the jumps. The longer you survive, the faster Bolt runs.

Controls

  • Jump: tap the game, or press Space, ↑ or W.
  • Higher jump: hold the button down. Release early for a short hop.
  • Double jump: tap again while in the air (once per jump).
  • Pause: P or Esc, or the pause button. The game also pauses if you switch tabs.
  • Sound on/off: M or the speaker button.

Difficulty levels

Choose a level on the start screen before you play. Your choice is remembered, and each level keeps its own best score.

  • Easy: slower running, more space between obstacles, and drones only appear after a while. Good for beginners and younger players.
  • Medium: the standard challenge.
  • Hard: starts fast, speeds up quickly, and brings vents and drones in almost straight away.

In 2-player mode, both players race on the level you picked.

2-player mode

Race a friend on the same phone, tablet or computer. Choose 2 Players on the start screen, and the screen splits into two lanes: Player 1 (teal robot) on top and Player 2 (orange robot) below.

  • Player 1: press W, or tap the top half of the game.
  • Player 2: press ↑, or tap the bottom half.
  • Both players get exactly the same course, so it's a fair race.
  • A 3-second countdown starts each race. If one player crashes, the other keeps running until they crash too.
  • The higher score wins, and the game keeps count of matches won until you close the page.

On a phone, hold it between you and give each player one half of the screen. Both players can tap at the same time.

Obstacles

  • Crates: jump over them. Some come in pairs, so jump early.
  • Vents: tall pipes that need a proper, held jump.
  • Gaps: the spaces between buildings. Falling in ends the run. Use your double jump if you jumped too early.
  • Drones: they hover at head height. Stay on the ground and run underneath, and don't jump into them.

Power-ups and jump pads

Power-ups float just above head height between obstacles, so a small hop grabs them.

  • Shield (blue): protects you from one crash. If you hit an obstacle, the obstacle breaks instead. If you fall into a gap, you bounce back up.
  • Magnet (red): pulls nearby orbs towards you for 8 seconds.
  • 2× points (gold): distance and orbs count double for 8 seconds.
  • Jump pads (green springs): run over one to launch high into the sky. A trail of orbs follows your path, and there is always room to land.

Robot colours

Bolt starts out teal. Score 1,500 on any level to unlock Violet, 3,000 for Lime and 6,000 for Gold. Pick your colour under the game.

Scoring

You earn points for every bit of distance you cover. Each energy orb adds 25 points, and collecting orbs quickly one after another builds a combo of up to ×5. Every 1,000 points you get a milestone banner. Your best score is saved on your device, separately for each difficulty.

Tips

  • Save your double jump. It's your emergency button for a mistimed jump.
  • Watch for drones before you jump. A drone straight after a crate means a short hop, not a big one.
  • Jump late over gaps. Take off near the edge to land safely on the next roof.
  • Don't chase every orb. Surviving is worth more than one bonus.
