How to play Shafkat Runner

Shafkat Runner is a free endless runner you can play in your browser. Bolt the robot runs automatically across a neon city skyline, and your job is to time the jumps. The run gets faster the further you go.

  • Jump over crates and vents, and across the gaps between rooftops.
  • Hold the jump for more height, or tap quickly for a short hop.
  • Double jump once in mid-air to save a bad jump or clear a wide gap.
  • Drones hover at head height: stay on the ground and run underneath them.
  • Energy orbs give bonus points.
  • 2-player mode: race a friend on the same phone or computer. You both get exactly the same course, and the higher score wins.
