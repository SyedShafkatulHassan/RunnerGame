Shafkat Runner: Free Endless Runner Game Online

Shafkat Runner is a free endless runner game you play right in your browser, with no download and no sign-up. Bolt, a small rooftop robot, runs on his own across a neon city skyline at night. Your job is to time the jumps. The further you go, the faster the run gets. It works on phones, tablets and computers.

How to play

  • Jump over crates and vents, and across the gaps between rooftops.
  • Hold the jump for more height, or tap quickly for a short hop.
  • Double jump once in mid-air to save a bad jump or clear a wide gap.
  • Drones hover at head height: stay on the ground and run underneath them.
  • Energy orbs give 25 points each. Grab them quickly in a row for a combo of up to ×5.
  • Jump pads launch you high into the sky through a trail of orbs.
  • Power-ups: a Shield saves you from one crash or fall, a Magnet pulls orbs to you, and 2× points doubles everything for 8 seconds.

On a computer, press Space, ↑ or W. On a phone, tap the screen. Read the full guide and high-score tips.

2-player mode: race a friend on one device

Choose 2 Players and the screen splits into two lanes. Player 1 presses W or taps the top half, and Player 2 presses ↑ or taps the bottom half. You both run exactly the same course, so it is a fair race, and the higher score wins. It is a quick 2-player game for friends, siblings or a break at work.

Features

  • Free to play in any modern web browser, on phone, tablet or computer.
  • Single-player and local 2-player modes.
  • Three difficulty levels: Easy for beginners and kids, Medium for a normal challenge, and Hard for fast reflexes. Each level keeps its own best score.
  • Short runs of one to three minutes, good for a quick break.
  • Your best score is saved on your device, and beating score targets unlocks new robot colours.
  • The sky changes from dusk to night to dawn the further you run.
  • Light and fast: no installs, no account and no heavy downloads.

Frequently asked questions

Is Shafkat Runner free?

Yes. The game is completely free to play in your browser.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open the page and press 1 Player or 2 Players to start. Nothing is installed on your device.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Tap to jump and hold for a higher jump. In 2-player mode, each player taps their own half of the screen.

Is there an easy mode?

Yes. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard on the start screen before you play. Easy is slower and gives you more space between obstacles; Hard starts fast and brings drones in almost straight away.

How do I get a higher score?

Save your double jump for emergencies, take off close to the edge of each gap, and don't jump when a drone is coming. See the how to play guide for more tips.

Is my score saved?

Your best score is stored in your own browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server.
