Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short version

Shafkat Runner has no accounts and no server of its own. We do not collect your name, email or any personal information through the game.

Data stored on your device

To save your progress, the game uses your browser's local storage. This stores your best score, number of runs and sound setting on your own device only. This data is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can learn more, and choose your ad settings, on Google's page How Google uses information from sites that use its services, or turn off personalised ads in Google Ad Settings.

Third-party services

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance.
  • Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Children

Shafkat Runner is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
